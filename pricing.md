# Nomi pricing

Start with Free. Open **Settings → Billing** in Nomi to choose or manage your plan.

- **Free:** Get started with daily limits on answers, web searches, and voice.
- **Pro ($12/month or $120/year):** Get higher daily limits.
- **Max ($50/month or $500/year):** Get the highest daily limits.

Students get **25% off monthly Pro** by verifying their school email in the app.
