# Nomi pricing

Start with Free. Open **Settings → Billing** in Nomi to see current prices and which plans are available to you.

- **Free:** Get started with daily limits on answers, web searches, and voice.
- **Pro:** Get higher daily limits.
- **Max:** Get the highest daily limits.

Eligible students can verify their school email in the app for a discount on monthly Pro when offered.
